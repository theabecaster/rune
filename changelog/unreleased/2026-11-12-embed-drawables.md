- **Embedding: `LayerHost` takes its drawables as a window does, and counts its frames.** A surface the GPU refuses to configure no longer panics in wgpu's default handler: `from_core_animation_layer` returns the error, and a refusal on a later resize is printed to stderr, each time one is refused, and costs frames until a resize configures it again. A frame that gets no drawable waits as a window's does (a covered surface on a wait that doubles to 2 s, an outdated one after it is configured again) and `EmbedFrame::wake_after_secs` carries that wait, so a host whose drawable stays lost stops reporting `animating`. `LayerHost::surface_stats` and `Runtime::surface_stats` report the surface's redraws, presents and misses, as they do for windows and overlays.
