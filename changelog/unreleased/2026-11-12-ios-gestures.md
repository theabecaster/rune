- **iOS: two fingers pinch and turn.** A `RuneView` recognizes a pinch and a rotation, both at once, and sends them where a trackpad's go: `El::on_magnify` hears `MagnifyEvent`s (scale by `1.0 + delta`) and `El::on_rotate` hears `RotateEvent`s (degrees, positive counterclockwise), each at the centre of the fingers with its phase. When either begins, the touch the view held is cancelled, so a pinch never also pans or presses.
