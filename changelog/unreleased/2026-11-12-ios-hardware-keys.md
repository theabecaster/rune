- **iOS: a hardware keyboard works as on a Mac.** Arrows, Return, Escape, Tab, Delete, Home, End, Page Up and Down and the function keys reach the runtime with their modifiers, a held key repeats, and a character typed with ⌘ is `Key::Command`, so ⌘A, ⌘C, ⌘X, ⌘V and ⌘Z edit a field and an app's chords match. With no field focused a typed character arrives as `Key::Text` for `on_key`. On iOS ⌘ is the primary modifier and the Mac's text conventions apply (⌥ moves by word, ⌘ by line, the Control-letter bindings). The three-finger copy, cut and paste gestures do the same as the shortcuts.
