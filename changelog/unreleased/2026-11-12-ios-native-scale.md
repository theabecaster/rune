- **iOS: a `RuneView` draws at the screen's own scale.** A `UIView` on a `CAMetalLayer` starts at one pixel a point whatever the screen, so the view drew a third of an iPhone's pixels each way and the system stretched them: text and edges were soft. It takes its window's screen's `nativeScale` when it joins the window. On an iPhone 18 Pro at 3x a frame of `examples/web-hello` takes 1.7 ms of CPU (median; 2.8 ms at worst over 230 frames of dragging).
