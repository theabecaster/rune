- **iOS: `App::run` and `rune::run` start an app on an iPhone or iPad, and `rune::ios::view` hands a UIKit or SwiftUI app a view that draws one.** Both draw in a `RuneView`, a `UIView` on a `CAMetalLayer` over `embed::LayerHost`. A `CADisplayLink` asks for 120 Hz and ticks only while something moves: it rests when a frame says nothing is, and starts again on a touch, a resize, a wake from another thread or the runtime's next timer, so an idle app draws nothing. It stops in the background, where Metal refuses drawables, and follows the system's light or dark appearance. A finger taps, drags and pans with momentum as on the web. `App::run` makes the app delegate, the window scene and the window in code, so an app's `Info.plist` needs only an empty `UIApplicationSceneManifest`; it never returns on iOS. `scripts/ios-app` bundles a built binary, signs it (ad hoc for a simulator, with a development profile for a device), installs it and launches it (`IOS.md`). Not there yet: the soft keyboard and text input, safe-area insets (`Cx::safe_area` is zero), more than one finger, VoiceOver, the clipboard and `on_ready`.
