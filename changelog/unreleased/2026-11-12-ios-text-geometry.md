- **iOS: the keyboard knows where a field's characters are.** A `RuneView`'s text input answers UIKit's questions about geometry from the focused field's laid-out lines: the caret's rect at any position, the position nearest a point, a range's first rect, and the position a line up or down. The space bar's trackpad moves the caret through the text, and the keyboard's bubbles sit at the text they are about. While a composition is open, and for a password, the answers stay the caret's own rect.
