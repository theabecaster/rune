- **iOS: a finger's move reaches the screen at the next refresh after it arrives.** The display link rested after every frame with nothing moving, so each move of a dragging finger started it again, and a started link's first tick comes late. The link now keeps ticking while a finger or a two-finger gesture is down, and a tick draws only when something may have changed since the last frame (input, a wake from another thread, the runtime's timer, or motion still running), so a finger held still draws nothing. On an iPhone 18 Pro, with a 60 Hz pointer, the time from a move's timestamp to the refresh that shows it fell from 32.5 ms to 23.5 ms (medians); of what is left, 14.4 ms is the system delivering the move, the frame is drawn 29 µs after delivery, and it shows 9 ms later, at the next refresh.
