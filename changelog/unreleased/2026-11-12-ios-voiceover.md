- **iOS: VoiceOver, Switch Control and Voice Control read a `RuneView`.** The view is an accessibility container whose elements come from the runtime's accessibility tree, flattened as UIKit reads a screen: each control, piece of text and image is one element with its label, value, hint, traits (button, link, header, selected, not enabled, adjustable, toggle) and frame, and the text inside a control is its name. A double tap presses or focuses, a swipe on a slider steps it, a three-finger scroll scrolls the scroll view around the element, and landing on something scrolled out of view scrolls it back. `Tx::announce` and live regions are spoken. The runtime builds no tree until a client asks. `AccessibilityLink::act` queues an assistive client's action, as `activate` does its first request.
