- **iOS: input and VoiceOver follow `Cx::zoom`.** A `RuneView` gave the runtime its touches, gestures, pointer, scrolls and long presses in the view's points, and VoiceOver the tree's frames in the app's, so in an app that zooms (as one following `Cx::system_text_scale` does) a press landed off its target and VoiceOver outlined the wrong place. Positions are now divided by the zoom on the way in and accessibility frames multiplied by it on the way out, as a desktop window does. A text field's menu now points at its caret and follows it, so it stays above the keyboard that focusing the field brings up.
