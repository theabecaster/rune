- **`Cx::safe_area` reports an iPhone's status bar, home indicator and corners, and a view's memos rebuild when it changes.** The insets are the runtime's own now (`Runtime::set_safe_area`, in top/right/bottom/left points): an iOS view sets them from its `safeAreaInsets` and when they change (a rotation, a view that moves under the status bar), and a web canvas from the page's, as before. A view under `Cx::zoom` reads them in its own points. They are part of what a `memo` scope hashes, so a memo that reads them needs nothing in its version. Zero on the desktop and headless.
