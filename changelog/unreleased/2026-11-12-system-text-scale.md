- **`Cx::system_text_scale` is the system's text size setting.** How much larger or smaller than its default the user's text size is: iOS's Dynamic Type, which a `RuneView` reads at launch and follows when it changes; 1 on platforms that report none. A view scales itself with `cx.zoom(cx.system_text_scale())`, or only its type. A change re-runs the view and the memos that read it; a host sets it with `Runtime::set_system_text_scale`.
