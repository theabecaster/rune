- **Embedding: `LayerHost::with_system_links` opens a handler's links in the system.** An embedded view's host that wants `Tx::open_url` to open links as an app's own window does says so with the builder, beside `with_system_clipboard`, `with_popup_menus` and `with_file_dialogs`; others still open nothing and take the checked links with `Runtime::take_url_requests`. An iOS `RuneView` asks for it, so its links open as before.
