- **iOS: `AppHandle` reaches a `RuneView`'s app.** `AppHandle::update` and `AppHandle::wake` from any thread wake the view, and the next frame runs the updates and `poll`, as a window's event loop does; before, they were never run. `AppHandle::is_visible` is false while the app is in the background. Requests to show, move or quit a window have nothing to act on in a view and are dropped.
